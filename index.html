Good to know

Something else on your mind? Send us a message and a person at the track will answer.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need to book?

No. You can arrive and drive whenever the track is open. Booking ahead holds a slot for you, and groups should always book.

What is a double kart?

A two-seater kart, so two people ride together. It’s a good choice for a parent with a younger child.

How does Friday Madness work?

Every Friday, pay for 10 minutes and get an extra 5 minutes free. It counts for single and double karts. Choose 10 minutes on a Friday in the booking form and it’s added for you.

When is the track open?

The track runs during Highveld Mall trading hours. Send us a WhatsApp on 082 321 7804 to check today’s times.

How much does a party or year-end function cost?

Every group is different, so we quote per event. Send an enquiry with your date and number of drivers and we’ll come back to you.

How do I hear about specials?

Join the MC Karting WhatsApp group or follow us on Facebook.
