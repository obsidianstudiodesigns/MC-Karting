What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us, such as:

  • your name, cellphone number and, if you add it, your email address
  • the date and time you want to race, the kart you choose and how many karts you need
  • for events: the type of event, number of drivers, their age group and your company or school name
  • anything you add in a message, such as a note about a first-time driver or a child riding in a double kart

How the forms on this website work

The booking and event forms do not store or send anything to us by themselves. When you press a send button, the form opens WhatsApp or your email app with a message already written. Nothing reaches us unless you send that message yourself. The price shown on the booking form is worked out on your own device.

Why we use it

  • to confirm your booking and answer your questions
  • to plan and quote for parties, Grand Prix races, endurance races, corporate events and year-end functions
  • to run sessions safely on the day
  • to keep records we are required to keep by law

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We only send you specials or updates if you ask for them, for example by joining our WhatsApp group.

Children

Bookings and enquiries are made by adults. When a parent or guardian books for a child, we only use the child’s details, such as their age group, to plan the session safely.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to in order to run your booking or event, or when the law requires it. The services we use to talk to you, such as WhatsApp, Facebook (Meta) and email providers, process messages under their own privacy policies.

The WhatsApp group

If you join the MC Karting WhatsApp group, your name and number are visible to other members, as with any WhatsApp group. You can leave the group at any time.

This website, cookies and third parties

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. To load, your browser fetches files from these providers, which may record your IP address and browser details in their standard server logs:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typeface
  • jsDelivr, which supplies the icons
  • Google Maps, which shows the map in the “Find the track” section

Links to Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Maps and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and enquiry messages only as long as we need them to deal with your request. Records of payments and invoices are kept for as long as tax and other laws require, usually five years, and then deleted.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, including keeping it on password-protected devices and accounts and only sharing it when needed.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • object to us using it
  • withdraw consent you have given

Contact us on the details above and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
