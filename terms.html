Prices

Prices on this website are in South African rand, per kart, and match the price list at the track on the date above. Lap counts are a guide only and depend on how busy the track is. MC Karting may change prices and specials at any time. The price that applies is the one confirmed by MC Karting for your booking.

Friday Madness

On Fridays, when you pay for a 10-minute session you get an extra 5 minutes free, on single and double karts. MC Karting may change or end the special at any time.

Bookings

  • Sending a booking from this website is a request. Your slot is confirmed only when MC Karting replies to confirm it.
  • We’ll do our best to give you the time you ask for. If it’s taken, we’ll offer the closest open slot.
  • Please arrive in good time for your session. Late arrival may shorten your time on track.
  • If you can’t make it, let us know on WhatsApp as early as you can so we can give the slot to someone else.

Parties, Grand Prix races and functions

Event formats have driver limits: kiddies birthday parties need at least 10 drivers, a Mini Grand Prix is for 4 to 20 drivers and a Super Grand Prix is for 12 drivers or more. Prices, deposits and times for events are agreed with MC Karting when you book and are confirmed in writing on WhatsApp or by email.

Safety on the track

  • Everyone driving or riding must follow the instructions of the MC Karting staff and marshals at all times.
  • Staff may set age, height or other limits for drivers and passengers, and may stop anyone from driving if it is unsafe.
  • Karting is a motorsport with risks. You may be asked to sign an indemnity form before driving. A parent or guardian signs for anyone under 18.
  • Wear closed shoes and tie back long hair and loose clothing.
  • No one may drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
